How this channel works

DetectA scanner agent searches public sources every 3 hours for AI incidents, near-misses and threat patterns. Every item carries its event date and source link.
ReceiveReports from the public, developers, deployers and agencies enter the same pipeline through the report form.
AssessMY-AISafe officers classify severity, identify the AIGE principles affected and the lifecycle stage of the system, and record the root cause.
ContainContainment actions are coordinated with the responsible developer, deployer or sector lead.
ShareLessons learned are published on closed items so the ecosystem can act on them.
BasisNational AI Action Plan 2026–2030 initiative E12 (AI Trust Function); National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics (AIGE, 2024); ISO/IEC 5338:2023 AI lifecycle.
